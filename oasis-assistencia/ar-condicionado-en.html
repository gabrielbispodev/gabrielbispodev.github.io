Frequently asked questions.

What capacity is right for my space?

It depends on the size, sun exposure, how many people use the space and any heat-producing equipment. We do this calculation on the site visit before recommending a unit.

How often should I get maintenance?

You can clean the filters yourself regularly. A full deep clean and technical check-up are recommended periodically, depending on use; for businesses, the maintenance plan sets the schedule.

Do you install units bought from another store?

Yes. We assess the location and the model and do the full installation with a performance test.

Do you serve areas outside the city?

Yes. We serve the city and the whole metro area.
