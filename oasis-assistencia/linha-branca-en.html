Appliance installation.

A bad install voids the warranty and wastes water and energy.

  • Washers and washer-dryers: leveling, plumbing and electrical.
  • Dishwashers and built-in ovens.
  • Fridges and freezers: placement, ventilation and testing.
  • Usage walkthrough and first start-up.

Appliance repair.

A clear diagnosis before replacing any part.

  • Fridges that don't cool, make noise or build up too much ice.
  • Washers that don't drain, don't spin or leak.
  • Control boards, sensors and thermostats.
  • Residential and commercial service.

Parts for air conditioners and appliances.

The right part avoids makeshift fixes.

  • Air conditioning: boards, sensors, capacitors, motors, blower wheels and filters.
  • Appliances: pumps, valves, thermostats and boards.
  • Check availability by your unit's model.
