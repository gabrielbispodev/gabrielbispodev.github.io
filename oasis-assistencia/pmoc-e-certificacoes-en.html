Maintenance plan.

A preventive maintenance, operation and control plan for your HVAC systems.

  • Recommended for buildings and shared air-conditioned spaces, and required by many local codes.
  • Equipment inventory, maintenance routines and a record of every visit.
  • A licensed technician in charge and reports ready for inspections.
  • Helps keep indoor air quality up and breakdowns down.

Permits.

Licensed, insured, permitted work.

  • Every job is registered under a licensed professional.
  • Important for larger installs, construction and maintenance contracts.
  • Pulled by Oasis for the job you hire us for.

OSHA and EPA 608 certifications.

Safety for our technicians and your business.

  • OSHA: electrical safety and safe work practices.
  • Fall protection: work at height, common when installing condensers.
  • EPA 608: certified refrigerant handling.
  • Documentation available for onboarding at companies and buildings.

Frequently asked questions.

Does my business need a maintenance plan?

Many buildings and shared air-conditioned spaces are required to keep one, depending on local codes. We review your case on the site visit.

What's included in the plan?

Equipment inventory, maintenance routines and frequency, visit records and a licensed technician in charge.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We pull the permits for the work we do.

Is your team certified to work at my business?

Yes: OSHA safety training, fall protection and EPA 608 refrigerant certification, with documentation for onboarding.
